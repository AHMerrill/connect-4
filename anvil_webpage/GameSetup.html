📖 How to Play

Connect 4 - Longhorn Edition

Getting Started

Challenge an AI opponent by selecting your preferred model (CNN or Transformer) and choosing to play as Player 1 (Burnt Orange) or Player 2 (White).

Objective

Be the first to connect four of your chips in a row - horizontally, vertically, or diagonally!

Gameplay

  • Player 1 (Burnt Orange) always moves first
  • Click on any column to drop your chip
  • Chips fall to the lowest available position
  • Players alternate turns until someone wins or the board fills up

Tips

  • Control the center column - it's part of the most winning combinations
  • Watch for diagonal threats - they're easy to miss!
  • Try to create multiple winning threats at once
  • Block your opponent while building your own connections

Fun Fact

Connect 4 is a solved game - with perfect play, the first player can always win. Can you beat our AI?

🤖 About the Models

AI Opponents

🧠 CNN (Convolutional Neural Network)

An AlphaZero-style residual CNN that treats the board like an image.

  • Uses 2D convolutions to scan the 6x7 board
  • Residual blocks detect patterns like lines, threats, and blocks
  • Trained on 700K+ game positions from MCTS simulations
  • Outputs a policy (best column) and value (win probability)

⚡ Transformer

A Vision Transformer architecture adapted for Connect 4.

  • Uses attention mechanisms to analyze relationships between all 42 positions
  • 6 transformer blocks with 8 attention heads each
  • Trained on 1.4M+ positions (with mirror augmentation)
  • Achieves 77% policy accuracy and 92% top-2 accuracy

How They Were Trained

Both models learned by studying moves from Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) - an algorithm that simulates thousands of games to find the best moves. The models learned to predict what MCTS would play.
